About me

Abdalla Kareem is an Indonesian film director and cinematographer based in Depok. Driven by a deep curiosity for visual storytelling and directing techniques, he formalized his passion through his education at SMK Industri Kreatif Ghama Caraka. As a director, Kareem focuses on character-driven narratives, consistently exploring the nuances of human emotion and thought.

His directorial work has already gained academic and industry recognition, including an award at the Universitas Indonesia Vocational Program Broadcasting Festival. Alongside directing, Kareem actively gains industry experience, notably serving as the Second Assistant Camera (2nd AC) for the WeTV series Pacarku Jinny.

Growing coping and living

Genre: Drama/coming of age | Directed by Abdalla Kareem

Director's Statement

Sometimes there is guilt in taking a step back, and it messes with our minds so much. Being at a different stage in life compared to others our age can be frustrating, especially if one of those people is someone who means a lot to you. There is so much social judgment for not choosing/doing things the "right" way.

It feels like a crime to not want to be rich, or to choose not to follow the conventional path in life. I think it is entirely possible for humans to have different desires. Why can't we just live the way we want to? And if we find ourselves at a stage of loss, not knowing what to do, should we really have to feel guilty about it?

Beyond the surface

Genre: Drama/conversation
Written and Directed by Abdalla Kareem

Director's Statement

From childhood we are taught that setting goals shapes standard paths of development towards domestic status Social judgment for not following conventional pathways can feel oppressive. Sometimes standard rules are fundamentally broken and are constructed to maintain control rather than help development.Since childhood, we are taught that talking back to our elders is a sign of disobedience, warned that hell is reserved for that kind of person. We are told to listen—which isn't entirely wrong—but we are also taught to stand up for ourselves. Yet, the minute we do, we are punished. As children, we felt confused by this; it felt as though some authorities exist only to be obeyed, never questioned. But that isn't what family is.

For a long time, we follow these rules, until the day we finally break—the day some part of ourselves needs to let go. Sometimes, we pour that bottled up pain into negative things, and unfortunately, that reaction is the only part that gets judged. Once again, we question ourselves: 'Why do I keep making mistakes?' 'Why can't I do anything right?' The guilt is overwhelming and incredibly difficult to recover from. People accuse us of playing the victim, yet the only person we truly blame is ourselves, It makes us colder, more distant, and still we are labeled rude or heartless. We try so hard, but when nothing seems to work, we simply stop trying. It takes a long time to believe that anyone could genuinely care for us. It takes a lot of running and avoiding. But when we finally meet someone who takes the time to listen and understand, rather than preach or lecture, there is a profound sigh of relief.

Like many relationships, some things don't last just because they are perfect. Sometimes we meet people just to be a part of their journey, not to stay. But meeting that person changes the way we see the world. It gives us hope. It gives us a reason to keep going—and that is more than enough.

Pacarku Jinny

Genre: Drama/romance/fantasy
Directed by Anggy Umbara

synopsis

Lila adalah seorang jin menawan yang terdampar di bumi setelah kehilangan gelangnya yang merupakan alat transportasinya ketika menyelamatkan nyawa seorang laki-laki.Satu-satunya ingatan yang dia miliki hanyalah tato yang ada pada dada lelaki tersebut.

Dia pun harus berpacu dengan waktu untuk mendapatkan kembali gelangnya dalam waktu 100 hari atau menghadapi kebinasaan.Selama perjalanannya, Lila jatuh cinta dengan Andra, seorang produser, hanya untuk menemukan bahwa dia adalah lelaki misterius yang selama ini dicarinya. Akan tetapi, untuk mendapatkan kembali gelangnya berarti Andra akan mati.

Kini, dia pun dilema dan terbelah pada dua pilihan antara menyelamatkan hidup sendiri dan cinta dalam hidupnya. Lila harus membuat pilihan yang mustahil.Lantas, akankah Lila mengorbankan cintanya untuk menyelamatkan dirinya? Atau justru Lila mengambil risiko untuk bersama Andra, dan menghadapi konsekuensi paling besar?
